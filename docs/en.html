Features

Everything important in one calm place

Tools for hard moments and for everyday self-care. No pressure.

🛡️

Alert

Hold the SOS button and trusted people get a text message with your location. Ready messages for WhatsApp and Telegram, emergency call in one tap.

🎙️

Evidence

Background voice recorder, a vault for documents and injury photos, an incident journal with attachments. Each file gets a date, a SHA-256 fingerprint and coordinates.

🗝️

Privacy from prying eyes

PIN and fingerprint login, a duress PIN that opens a harmless notepad, quick exit and screenshot blocking.

🌿

Support

DBT and CBT cards, grounding, help with panic and flashbacks, a voice guide, breathing exercises and a safety plan.

📔

Journals

Mood check-ins with a chart, a dream diary, a schema-therapy modes diary, a thought record and a calendar with reminders.

⚖️

Rights under 18

Step-by-step guides for teenagers in Russia: if a passport was taken, if the police do not help, if child services do nothing.

Privacy

Your data belongs only to you

We cannot see anything you store in DTM — there are simply no servers for it to go to.

  • Recordings, documents and journals stay on the phone, encrypted with a key from Android's secure storage.
  • No sign-up, phone number or email.
  • Android cloud backup is disabled — data never goes to Google.
  • Backups are created only by you and encrypted with your password.
  • The code is open, so anyone can verify what the app does.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No ads, subscriptions or paid features.

Is there an English interface?

Not yet — the app is in Russian for now. English localization is planned.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

It is deleted with the app. Create a backup in settings and keep the file outside the phone.

Can recordings be used as evidence?

DTM records network time, coordinates and a SHA-256 fingerprint of each file and builds a report for a lawyer. Whether materials are accepted is decided by a court or investigators. Ask a lawyer.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — Android 8.0 and newer only.

Architecture & security

Zero-server: there is nowhere for your data to leak

DTM has no servers, accounts or analytics. Everything runs and is stored on the phone, and the source code is open for review.

🔑

Key in Android Keystore

An AES-256-GCM master key is generated on the device and kept in Android Keystore — hardware-backed on supported phones. It cannot be extracted.

🗄️

Everything encrypted

Files: AES-256-GCM with HKDF in 4 KB segments (Google Tink). Settings: AES-256-SIV for keys and AES-256-GCM for values. No plaintext copies on disk.

🔢

PIN

Stored only as a hash: random salt and 20,000 SHA-256 iterations. After 5 wrong attempts login is locked with a growing delay.

💾

Backups

Key derived from your password with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 150,000 iterations; data encrypted with AES-256-GCM in 64 KB chunks. Without the password nobody can open it, including the developer.

🧾

Evidence integrity

Each recording and photo gets a SHA-256 fingerprint, time from a network time server (SNTP) and coordinates. Changing a single byte changes the fingerprint.

🌐

Network only when needed

The app goes online only for accurate time, when sending SOS and when checking GitHub for updates. Android cloud backup and screenshots are blocked.

Important

  • DTM is a support tool. It helps but cannot guarantee safety.
  • SOS delivery is not guaranteed: it depends on network coverage, phone settings and SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram.
  • DTM does not replace a psychotherapist, psychologist or doctor and is not medical or legal advice.
  • Whether recordings, photos and reports are accepted as evidence is decided by a court or investigators. The fingerprint and time prove a file is unchanged, not that it will be accepted.
  • Laws on recording conversations differ between countries. Check what is allowed where you live.
  • Location may be inaccurate or unavailable. Without internet, device time is used instead of network time.
  • The software is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind (GNU GPL v3). The developers are not liable for consequences of its use.
